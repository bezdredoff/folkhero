# Art Reference Index

Status: research index. A link appearing here does **not** automatically make it an approved target.

## Reference labels

- **Evidence** — historical/museum/scholarly factual support.
- **Inspiration** — artistic treatment or mood only.
- **Target** — explicitly approved direction.
- **Anti-reference** — useful example of what to avoid.

## Confidence / chronology labels

Use:
- **P1** — primary-period or near-primary evidence;
- **P2** — scholarly/museum reconstruction tied to documented evidence;
- **E** — later ethnographic continuity; useful, but not assumed unchanged since the 16th century;
- **I** — modern artistic interpretation.

## Environment / Upper Berezina

### UNESCO — Berezinsky Biosphere Reserve
- Label: Evidence
- Confidence: modern ecology evidence
- URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/mab/berezinsky
- Use for: landscape mosaic, wetlands, peat bogs, mixed forest, lakes, watercourses, floodplain, meadow relationships.
- Note: modern protected ecology, not historical settlement evidence.

## Rural built environment

### Muzeum Wsi Lubelskiej
- Label: Evidence / E
- URL: https://skansen.lublin.pl/en/sightseeing/
- Use for: rural spatial vocabulary, road surfaces, farmstead logic, timber building relationships.
- Caveat: much material is later than the game period; date each individual object before borrowing details.

### Muzeum Wsi Opolskiej
- Label: Evidence / E
- URL: https://muzeumwsiopolskiej.pl/ekspozycja-stala/
- Use for: farmstead composition, relationship of home/barn/granary/livestock buildings, historical cultivated landscape.
- Caveat: different subregions and later chronology; use structural logic, not direct copying.

### Rural Tasks (1502), Sebastian Brant/Virgil woodcut
- Label: Evidence
- Confidence: P1, western/central European comparison
- URL: https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/from-the-reformations-to-the-thirty-years-war-1500-1648/rural-tasks-1502
- Use for: work activities, tools, fences, boats, livestock, blacksmithing and rural visual density around 1500.
- Caveat: not GDL/Belarus-specific; use as comparative period evidence only.

## International presentation / violence research

### CERO rating system
- Label: Production research
- URL: https://www.cero.gr.jp/en/publics/index/17/
- Note: CERO explicitly evaluates animated blood, mutilation/body-cutting, corpses, killing/wounding and horror among violence-expression categories.
- Production implication: keep extreme gore modular so rating/localization changes do not require creature redesign.

### CERO Code of Ethics (2025 revision)
- Label: Production research
- URL: https://www.cero.gr.jp/en/relays/download/3/78/49/289/?file=%2Ffiles%2Flibs%2F289%2F%2F20250327114356889.pdf
- Note: excessive focus on violence can affect rating/eligibility.

### China publishing research
- Label: Production research
- Source examples:
  - https://nikopartners.com/game-regulations-in-china-everything-you-need-to-know/
  - https://appinchina.co/services/game-publishing/content-restrictions-for-publishing-games-in-china/
- Note: secondary industry sources describe substantially stricter treatment of blood, corpses/skeleton imagery and excessive violence than Japan.
- Production implication: build blood, corpse persistence, exposed-bone presentation and death effects as replaceable/configurable layers.
- Warning: China approval practice changes and is not reducible to a stable checklist. Re-validate with a publishing partner before submission.
