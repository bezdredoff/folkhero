## Shared human system — F01–F03
Normal/mildly stylized human anatomy, ordinary bodies, shared humanoid rig where practical. Youth/adult/elderly appearances; thin/average/stockier builds without turning every role into an extreme physique.
Soft rounded facial planes, varied jaw/cheek/nose shapes and mild asymmetry. No heroic shoulders or fashion-model default.
Local residents include blond/light, fair/dark blond and chestnut hair; age-related gray. No exclusive group face/hair code. Children require their own age-appropriate proportions, not scaled-down adult bodies.
Simple work hairstyles/head coverings; clean-shaven, short and fuller beards as appropriate.
Profession reads through activity, posture and one useful prop more than a unique costume.
Group identities use scoped cultural clothing/biography details while bodies/materials are shared.

## Seasonal clothing system — F02/F04
| State | Shared concept direction |
|---|---|
| Warm season | Undyed linen foundation, simple belt; trousers for male reference; female reference includes historically checked lower garment/apron and head covering |
| Cool/rain | Wool cloth svita/sermyaga or appropriate outer layer, practical head covering; wet material state rather than a newly designed costume |
| Winter | Sheepskin coat, warmer head covering, mittens, leg/foot insulation; preserve useful movement |
Wool cloth (woven textile) and sheepskin (hide with fleece) are different materials and geometry responses.
Winter baseline: fleece inward, small exposed fleece at selected edges; exact cut/fastenings remain research tasks. R01 supports period sheepskin/wool use, not every proposed cut.
Wild-animal fur is reserved for researched individual details (hunter/selected garments); no automatic trophy pelt with head/paws.
Small amount of ornament, sometimes none. Select motif/placement from evidence; magical affinity meanings are fiction.
Repairs, fading, localized wear; no obligatory universal dirt.
Mitts and head/neck coverage plus broader clothing/leg silhouette communicate winter; do not merely add snow to a summer model.
Fur uses broad volumes/edge breaks without strand geometry.

## Footwear and headwear candidates

First pair: simple leather footwear and foot/leg wraps as candidate. Bast footwear is an additional researched option. No shoe type assigned universally or exclusively by ethnicity.
R04 is catalogue-only, not a chosen shoe pattern; final child briefs must include dated examples or explicitly accepted extrapolation.

### Smith details
No bodybuilder anatomy. Linen work foundation; protective leather apron is a candidate, not a sourced fact yet.
Localized work staining/scorch wear. Street winter coat can be removed near hearth as appropriate.
Separate hearth, bellows, historically selected anvil and tools; no fantasy forging setup by default.

### Healer details
Basket, cloth pouches, a few vessels, mortar and drying space as candidates; object forms need references.
Show ordinary work first, rare restrained magic in a separate state. No masses of glowing charms.
Home and travel identities are identical. Individual knowledge does not imply ethnic supernatural ability.

### Ornament worker details
Mother remains an established weaver at home. Daughter has her own competent practice; exact age, motif set and tools open.
Home/work, departure and winter variants; compact sewing/ornament tool bundle and useful cloth, not whole household contents.
Magical equipment application can involve the smith and other crafts; precise mechanic does not follow directly from real textile technique.

## Player heroes — G01/G02
First reference concepts are adult men; this does not lock character creation to male-only or add a customization system.
Both belong to the home community; local rural clothing/materials are the foundation.

| Feature | Warrior | Vedun |
|---|---|---|
| Age | ~35–40 | ~40–50 |
| Build | Strong, moderately broad, normal proportions | Leaner, not frail or stooped by default |
| Face | Short beard, composed | Longer beard, attentive |
| Silhouette | Compact clothing mass plus clear weapon | Longer clothing silhouette plus staff |
| Foundation | Linen + substantial wool outer layer, selected retained service belongings | Linen + practical longer wool outer layer for rural/forest life |
| Equipment | Separate axe and spear variants | Primarily wooden working staff, restrained ritual/herb kit |
| Metal | Weapon head/point and necessary fittings | Small work knife/minimal fittings where justified |
| Baseline armor | No metal armor; quilted layer is research candidate | No armor |
No metal shoulder/forearm/shin armor by default. No crystal staff, horned headwear or excessive pendants. Staff grip and ornament are coherent with function.
Military experience reads through posture, weapon handling and maintained possessions.
Winter uses wool/sheepskin while preserving combat arm movement, hem clearance, weapon grip and readability.
Wood and cloth can bear ornaments/effects; metal frames are not required.
Rare future gear may visibly change protection; do not design MMO escalation.
Source gap: chosen late-XVI axe/spear/staff fittings and protective garment construction. R05 excluded earlier axe match remains excluded.
