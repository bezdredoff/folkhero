# Moodboard 00 — Global Visual Foundation

Status: first-pass brief for reference collection and concept generation.

## Intent

Establish a shared visual grammar for Folkhero before producing class, creature or biome-specific concepts.

Working phrase:

> **Grounded dark-fairytale stylized realism in a late-16th-century Belarusian / Ruthenian / Lithuanian / Polish material world.**

## What the board must communicate

- realistic human anatomy;
- ordinary people before fantasy archetypes;
- damp, restrained dark-fairytale atmosphere;
- historically plausible clothing/tools/buildings;
- clean natural color used selectively;
- white/red textile accents;
- supernatural elements emerging from the same landscape and culture;
- no generic fantasy visual shorthand.

## Target qualities

### Human form
- normal humanoid proportions;
- animation-library friendly;
- believable clothing layers;
- expressive silhouette without deformation.

### Surface/material treatment
- painterly/game-readable rather than photoreal;
- visible textile weave, wood grain, iron wear and dampness at useful scale;
- limited micro-detail;
- strong large-value grouping for isometric camera.

### Light
- soft overcast daylight as a common baseline;
- mist, shafts of clean light and reflected water used selectively;
- night scenes should preserve gameplay readability;
- supernatural light should be rare enough to remain meaningful.

### Color
Base:
- peat brown;
- smoke gray;
- wet timber;
- muted linen;
- iron;
- muddy green;
- desaturated blue-gray.

Clean/living accents:
- meadow green;
- clean-water blue/green;
- fresh leaf color;
- straw/gold sunlight;
- textile white;
- textile red.

### Avoid
- constant orange/teal cinematic grading;
- monochrome mud-brown grimdark;
- ubiquitous glowing runes;
- fantasy plate armor on ordinary people;
- giant shoulder pieces;
- excessive Celtic/Norse visual shorthand;
- random embroidery used without provenance;
- pristine costume-drama cleanliness in rural spaces.

## Reference families

### Evidence first
Collect:
- period paintings, engravings, inventories and surviving objects;
- museum reconstructions with documentation;
- Belarusian/Lithuanian/Polish ethnographic collections, clearly date-labeled;
- open-air museums for structural rural vocabulary, with chronology caveats.

### Artistic inspiration
Useful starting comparisons:
- The Witcher series/games — regional dark-fairytale atmosphere, but avoid copying its genericized visual vocabulary;
- The Blood of Dawnwalker — contemporary grounded dark-fantasy presentation;
- Kingdom Come: Deliverance II — realistic human scale, villages, material wear and production-friendly proportions; chronology/region differ;
- Królowa Bona (1980) — early/mid-16th-century Polish/GDL court material as a costume reference, especially for later urban/noble layers;
- Epitafium dla Barbary Radziwiłłówny — useful for Polish-Lithuanian elite visual language;
- old Polish Wiedźmin adaptation — rough regional folklore flavor;
- Volkodav — selective dark-fairytale/ethnic atmosphere only;
- Cossacks — broad regional military/costume inspiration, never factual evidence.

## Approval test

A proposed Folkhero image passes this board if:
1. people look plausibly human and retargetable;
2. removing all magic still leaves a recognizable regional historical world;
3. the image is dark without being colorless;
4. bright natural color has a reason;
5. folklore elements feel local rather than imported fantasy;
6. the scene remains readable at isometric gameplay scale.
