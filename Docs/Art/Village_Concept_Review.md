## Acceptance semantics

- Selected exploration: user accepted direction or continued directly after reviewed object; does not approve historical construction, scale, view consistency or production readiness.

- Superseded/rejected: retain for lineage and avoid repeating mistakes.
- Unreviewed archive: bridge image found among generated sources, but no corresponding approval in available discussion; never promote automatically.
- Provisional village illustration: archived after user requests documentation; accepted terrain schematic remains authoritative.
