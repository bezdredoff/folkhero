# Early Access and Long-Term Support

Status: v0.1.

## Business model

Folkhero is a premium buy-to-play game.

Purchasing the game includes the core gameplay/progression content.

Do not sell:
- power;
- ornaments;
- loot;
- energy;
- XP/progression acceleration.

Potential later paid content:
- substantial expansions;
- optional cosmetics.

## Lead platform

Early Access launches on PC first.

Mobile architecture is considered from the beginning, but iOS/Android launch comes later after:
- controls;
- networking;
- performance;
- UI;
- save/progression;

are proven.

## First Early Access release

The first public EA release should be a **complete small chapter**, not an interrupted technical demo.

Target:
- 1 region;
- 1 complete Act I;
- ~2–3 hours mandatory main story;
- several additional hours of side content/farming/replay;
- Warrior;
- Vedun;
- co-op from launch;
- persistent character progression;
- ornament system;
- status/reaction combat;
- procedural wilderness/dungeons;
- weather/season variation;
- several bosses;
- meaningful community hub progression.

## Region strategy

Do not require 2–4 regions at EA launch.

One strong region is sufficient if it proves the reuse model.

The first region must support meaningful systemic variants such as:
- summer/baseline;
- rainy/autumn;
- winter;
- flood or equivalent wet extreme.

Not all variants need identical content volume, but they must change gameplay.

## Co-op requirement

Co-op is a launch pillar.

At minimum, first EA must support stable:
- solo;
- 2-player co-op.

Long-term target remains 1–4 players.

Do not promise 4-player production quality until proven.

## Progression persistence

Avoid planned progression wipes.

If save/schema changes require migration:
- migrate;
- version saves;
- communicate clearly.

A wipe should be a technical emergency, not a progression design loop.

## Content growth after EA launch

Grow toward the first full campaign target:
- 3 regions;
- 5 acts;
- ~8–10 hours mandatory story;
- ~12–15 hours typical first playthrough;
- ~10–12 bosses;
- ~15–20 main quests.

Growth should prioritize:
- new systemic combinations;
- new weather/season/world-state use;
- new enemy families;
- new ornaments/build options;
- new authored quest anchors;
- reuse of proven region/content grammar.

## Long-term support philosophy

The game may receive support for several years.

A strong update should ideally add something that also changes old content.

Examples:
- new affinity/systemic rule;
- new ornament family;
- new enemy modifier;
- new regional state;
- new world-state consequence;
- new class later;
- new boss grammar;
- new procedural event family.

Avoid updates that are only large piles of isolated assets.

## Expansion strategy

Paid expansions are appropriate for:
- major new region/campaign chapters;
- substantial new class/archetype packages;
- major systemic additions.

Cosmetic DLC may be used if it does not compromise cultural tone or progression clarity.

## Live-service caution

"Long-term support" does not require:
- free-to-play economy;
- battle pass;
- daily chores;
- seasonal currencies;
- FOMO progression.

The project should remain sustainable for a very small team.
